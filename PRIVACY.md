# Privacy policy

Phone Remote turns a phone into the remote control of an Android TV or projector, over your own Wi-Fi.

**What it collects:** nothing. There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

**What stays on the TV or projector**

- The phones you paired: the name you gave each phone, a random id for its browser, when it was paired and last
  used, and a key derived from its pairing token. Removing a phone deletes these.
- Settings: the device's name, projector or TV, where the QR card sits, the phone limit and the other switches on
  the app's screen.

Android's own backup (if the device uses one) may copy the settings. The paired phones' keys are excluded from
backup and never leave the device.

**What goes to a paired phone, on your network only:** which app is open, the list of apps and their icons; if you
switch them on, what is playing (title, artist, artwork, position) and a small picture of the screen; a
diagnostics report when a phone asks for one (device model, Android version, setup; no names or keys). Guest phones
never get the screen picture or the report.

**Photos:** pictures a phone sends to show are kept in the app's private storage only while the slideshow is
open, and deleted when it closes. They never leave your network.

**Accessibility service:** used to press keys, move the selection and type into the selected text box for paired
phones, and to know which app is in front. Screen content is not recorded or sent, except the optional screen
picture above.

**Notification access (optional):** used only to see which app is playing media and what. Notifications are not
read, kept or sent.

**Internet:** the app serves its page on the local network. Its only connection outside your network is the
optional **Check for updates** (off by default): once a day it asks `api.github.com` for the latest release of this
project. That request carries no information about you, your device or your phones; GitHub sees your public IP
address, as with any website.

**Children:** the app collects no data from anyone.

**Changes:** this policy lives in the project's repository; changes show in its history.

Questions: open an issue on the project's repository.
